The Mathematics of Boosting

$$F_M(x) = \sum_{m=1}^M \alpha_m h_m(x)$$

\(F_M(x)\) : The final strong ensemble model's prediction.

\(M\) : The total number of iterations (weak learners).

\(h_m(x)\) : The prediction of the \(m\)-th weak learner (e.g., a simple decision stump).

\(\alpha_m\) : The voting power (weight) assigned to the \(m\)-th learner. Models with higher accuracy get larger \(\alpha_m\) values.
